# CG33 host verification (2026-11-16)

CG32 host suites are rerun on the **CG33 modified source** with the updated
expectation: `Get_CG_Value(-30000, 23)` must exceed +400 mm, and
`Get_CG_Value(30000, 23)` must be below -400 mm with `CG_RESULT_OK`.

The previous +/-240 mm suppressor and `CG RANGE` OLED message must be absent.
The signed-filter deadband remains 3 AD, and the obsolete five-sample
accumulator remains unused/cleared in the sampling loop.

Results: see build console and the paired test-run log.

**Not tested**: armcc5/Keil rebuild, MCU flashing, real ADC timing, or field
repeatability/accuracy.
